ABOUT

Nestled along the emerald banks of the Kelani River

Seravelle Kitulgala is a refined sanctuary where eco-luxury meets timeless tranquility. Inspired by the rhythm of the rainforest and the purity of flowing water, Seravelle was created for the modern explorer—those who seek meaningful connection to nature, without compromising comfort or elegance.

✦

Here, every detail is curated to awaken your senses:

Wake to birdsong and mist-wrapped hills. Dine with forest-framed views. Sleep beneath the stars in quiet, bespoke luxury. At Seravelle, we invite you to pause, breathe, and belong.

From white-water adventure to riverside yoga, from jungle treks to candlelit forest dinners, Seravelle is more than a resort—it’s an experience. One rooted in sustainability, Sri Lankan heritage, and a spirit of calm discovery.
