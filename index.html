A 4-Star Garden Sanctuary in Kitulgala

Nestled along Police Station Road in Oruthota, Kitulgala (Postal Code: 71720), Seravelle Kithulgala is a peaceful 4-star hotel surrounded by verdant rainforest foliage, scenic mountain views, and quiet nature gardens.

Whether you are an adventure seeker visiting Kitulgala for world-famous Kelani River white water rafting, a family traveling with children and pets, or seeking delicious authentic Chinese dining, Seravelle welcomes you with genuine warmth.
