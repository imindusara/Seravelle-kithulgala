Amenities & Guest Services

Curated eco-luxury facilities along the scenic Kelani River: safe river bath by the lounge, signature restaurant dining, fully air-conditioned rooms with hot water, complimentary Wi-Fi, and guided rainforest adventures.

Comprehensive Services

What Makes Your Stay Comfortable

Every feature is designed to ensure peace of mind. Notice: Seravelle Kithulgala is an eco-nature garden retreat and does not feature a swimming pool.

🌊

Safe Kelani River Bath

Guests can enjoy safe, tranquil natural bathing in the pristine Kelani River, directly accessible by the hotel lounge.

❄️

A/C & Hot Water

Fully air-conditioned luxury rooms and family cabanas featuring en-suite bathrooms with continuous high-pressure hot water.

🅿️

Free Private Parking

Spacious, secure on-site parking lot free of charge for guest vehicles, safari jeeps, and tour vans.

🐕

Pet-Friendly Policy

Pets are warmly allowed! Bring your dogs or cats along to enjoy the fresh mountain air and open garden lawns.

🥢

Chinese Restaurant & Room Service

On-site restaurant serving authentic Chinese noodles, wok creations, and daily meals delivered straight to your room.

🛡️

24-Hour Security & Front Desk

On-site security personnel, perimeter gating, and responsive guest assistance available around the clock.

🌄

Garden & Mountain Views

Private balconies and peaceful garden seating overlooking Kitulgala’s lush green valleys and birdlife.

🧸

Child-Friendly Facilities

Safe garden play grounds, spacious multi-bed rooms, baby cots on request, and family dining options.

📶

High-Speed Wi-Fi

Reliable internet access across all rooms and public garden areas for connectivity and remote work.

♿

Accessibility Advisory

Wheelchair accessibility is not formally specified. Ground-floor rooms are available; please notify our team in advance.

Seravelle Kithulgala
