Travel Readiness

Pre-Arrival Packing Checklist

Kitulgala enjoys a pleasant tropical climate with refreshing rainforest breezes and occasional afternoon showers.

👟 Outdoor & Rafting Gear

  • • Quick-drying t-shirts and shorts for river rafting
  • • Secure water shoes or strap sandals
  • • Waterproof dry bag for phone, wallet, & camera
  • • Light rain jacket or compact umbrella
  • • Comfortable trainers for rainforest trails

🐾 Traveling With Pets

  • • Pet harness, leash, and favorite blanket
  • • Pet food and water travel bowl
  • • Up-to-date pet vaccination records
  • • Seravelle provides garden lawn play area and pet kits

🏨 Hotel Timings & Details

  • • Check-In: From 2:00 PM
  • • Check-Out: Until 11:00 AM
  • • Parking: Free private parking on-site
  • • Dining: Chinese restaurant & room service
  • • Swimming Pool: Note: No pool on property
Route Guide

Getting to Seravelle Kithulgala

Located conveniently on Police Station Road, Oruthota, Kitulgala, 71720, Sri Lanka.

From Colombo / Airport (CMB) by Car (2 to 2.5 Hours)

Take the Colombo–Hatton Highway (A7) via Avissawella and Ruwanwella. Turn onto Police Station Road in Oruthota, Kitulgala. Free private on-site parking is available upon arrival.

Scenic mountain drive • Free parking

From Kandy (Approx. 2 Hours)

Drive via Gampola and Nawalapitiya through lush tea country, descending towards the Kelani River valley of Kitulgala.

Tea country route

Private Hotel Vehicle Transfer

We arrange comfortable air-conditioned van and car transfers directly from any hotel in Colombo, Kandy, or the international airport directly to our hotel entrance.

Available on request ($65 flat)
