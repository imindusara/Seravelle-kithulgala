Guest FAQs

Important Details About Your Stay

Answers to key questions based on property specifications.

What is the hotel type and rating?

Seravelle Kithulgala is a 4-star boutique garden hotel in Kitulgala with a 5.0 average guest review score, situated in tranquil surroundings on Police Station Road, Oruthota.

What are the check-in and check-out times?

Check-in begins at 2:00 PM and check-out is until 11:00 AM (noon). If you require early check-in or late checkout, please inform us in advance.

Are pets allowed at Seravelle Kithulgala?

Yes, pets are warmly allowed! Our spacious gardens and nature paths make Seravelle Kithulgala a wonderful choice for travelers with four-legged companions.

Is there a swimming pool on the property?

There is no swimming pool on-site. Seravelle Kithulgala is designed as an eco-nature garden hotel. Natural river bathing and water activities at the scenic Kelani River are located just minutes away.

What dining options are available?

We have an on-site restaurant serving authentic Chinese cuisine along with breakfast and local dishes. Room service is also available for in-room or balcony dining.

Is parking available and what is the cost?

Free private parking is available on-site for all registered hotel guests and restaurant visitors.

What is the minimum stay and cancellation policy?

The minimum stay is 1 night. We offer free cancellation on direct bookings, though policies may vary slightly during peak holiday seasons.

What are the nightly room rates?

Nightly rates typically range from about $72.15 to $104 per night for our Deluxe Single Room with Balcony and Double Room. Group rates for our 6-bedroom villa (accommodating up to 10 guests) are also available.

Is the hotel wheelchair accessible?

Wheelchair accessibility is not formally specified. Ground-floor rooms with garden access can be arranged upon request. Please contact our front desk prior to booking so we can accommodate your needs.
