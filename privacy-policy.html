Privacy Policy & Hotel Terms of Stay

Seravelle Kithulgala, Police Station Road, Oruthota, Kitulgala, 71720, Sri Lanka. Transparent guidelines on reservations, free cancellation, pet policies, and guest rights.

1. Hotel Information & Legal Entity

Seravelle Kithulgala ("Seravelle Kithulgala", "we", "us", or "our") is a 4-star boutique garden hotel situated on Police Station Road, Oruthota, Kitulgala, 71720, Sri Lanka. This document sets out our privacy commitments, booking guidelines, pet policies, and guest safety regulations.

2. Hotel Reservation & Stay Policies

  • • Check-In Time: From 2:00 PM onwards.
  • • Check-Out Time: Until 11:00 AM (noon).
  • • Minimum Stay Requirement: 1 night minimum stay.
  • • Free Cancellation: Free cancellation is offered on direct reservations, though specific terms may vary with season and peak holiday availability.
  • • Nightly Room Rates: Standard room rates range from about $72.15 to $104 per night (plus 10% local taxes). Group rates apply for the 6-bedroom villa.
  • • Free Private Parking: Complimentary on-site parking is provided for all registered guests.
  • • Pet-Friendly Policy: Pets are allowed. Pet owners are requested to keep pets leashed in common garden areas and ensure pets are well-behaved.
  • • No Swimming Pool: Seravelle does not feature a swimming pool on-site; guests are invited to enjoy our nature gardens or natural Kelani River swimming spots nearby.
  • • Accessibility Advisory: Wheelchair accessibility is not officially specified. Ground-floor rooms with level garden access can be reserved by contacting the hotel in advance.

3. Personal Data Collection & Security

We collect essential information strictly necessary to process your reservation, provide room service, and accommodate special pet or dining requests:

  • Guest Identity: Full name, email address, telephone/WhatsApp contact, and country of residence.
  • Stay Preferences: Arrival timing, room selection (e.g. Deluxe Single or 6-Bedroom Villa), pet travel notifications, and Chinese cuisine dietary preferences.
  • Data Protection: Guest records are kept confidential and are never sold or shared with unauthorized commercial entities.

4. Cookie & Session Storage

Our website utilizes standard browser localStorage and essential session cookies to remember your travel dates, room choices, and cookie consent preferences. You can clear cookies at any time via your browser settings.

5. Inquiries & Data Rights

To update or request deletion of your reservation profile, or for questions regarding our 4-star hotel policies, please reach our front desk:
