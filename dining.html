Authentic Chinese Cuisine & Dining

Fresh wok-seared flavors, aromatic broths, and dim sum specialties served in our garden restaurant in Kitulgala, with full room service and free private parking.

On-Site Culinary Feature

Chinese Gastronomy in Kitulgala

Our on-site restaurant specializes in authentic Chinese cuisine, offering guests an exquisite and comforting culinary journey after a day of rainforest hiking or river rafting.

From handcrafted noodles, savory stir-fries, and fragrant fried rice to crispy spring rolls and seasonal vegetable dishes, every meal is prepared fresh with quality ingredients.
