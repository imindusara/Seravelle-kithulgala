Our Story in Kitulgala, Sri Lanka

A peaceful garden retreat on Police Station Road, Oruthota. Dedicated to nature, pet-friendly comfort, authentic Chinese dining, and Sri Lankan hospitality.

Police Station Road, Oruthota, Kitulgala

Where Rainforest Greenery Meets Warm Hospitality

Seravelle Kithulgala is a distinguished 4-star boutique hotel located in the heart of Kitulgala, Sri Lanka—world-renowned for its emerald hills, pristine rainforests, and the rushing waters of the Kelani River.

Set amidst expansive private gardens with sweeping views of the surrounding hills, our property provides a tranquil sanctuary. Guests can choose from comfortable individual rooms—such as our Deluxe Single Room with Balcony—or reserve the entire 6-bedroom villa retreat with 2 bathrooms to host family gatherings of up to 10 guests.

We take pride in our on-site Chinese cuisine restaurant, our pet-friendly grounds where pets are warmly welcomed, and our thoughtful amenities including air conditioning, 24/7 security, room service, and free private parking.
