The Seravelle Suite (Super Deluxe River View)

Our signature suite overlooking the emerald waters of the Kelani River (Room No. 04), offering elevated luxury, private river-view balcony, and bespoke hospitality.

Room Overview

The Seravelle Suite (Room No. 04) is our signature super deluxe accommodation, perfectly positioned overlooking the sparkling Kelani River and the lush tropical rainforest canopy of Kitulgala.

Take in majestic river vistas and morning mist from your spacious private balcony. Inside, enjoy handcrafted luxury bedding, quiet climate control, high-speed Wi-Fi, elegant en-suite bathroom with hot water, and bespoke in-room dining from our on-site Chinese restaurant.
